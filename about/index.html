About

Atlas Fit is made by Andronik Capital LLC, an independent software company based in Florida.

Andronik Capital LLC designs and publishes Atlas Fit, a strength-training log for iPhone and Apple Watch, and is its seller on the App Store.

Atlas Fit is built to keep your training private. There are no accounts and no servers: workouts are stored on your iPhone, and Apple Health data is read on your device. The privacy policy has the detail.

Contact

For help with the app, questions about privacy, or anything else, email support@atlas-fit.fit. The support page answers the most common questions.
