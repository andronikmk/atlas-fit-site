Atlas Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-17

Atlas (Atlas Fit on the App Store) is a strength-training log for iPhone and Apple Watch, published by Andronik Capital LLC. This policy explains what data Atlas handles, where it is kept and what you can do with it.

The short version

  • Atlas has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Everything you log stays on your iPhone, and on your Apple Watch during a workout. This version of Atlas has no iCloud sync.
  • Apple Health data never leaves your iPhone.
  • Atlas has no servers. It does not upload, sell or share your data.
  • The AI Coach runs on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence. Your messages are not sent anywhere.
  • Atlas has no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Atlas Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; Atlas never sees your payment details.

No account

Atlas has no accounts and no sign-in, so it never asks for your email address or a password. Earlier test builds offered sign-in. When Atlas updates from one of those builds, it keeps the profile details you entered on the device and deletes the old sign-in records.

What Atlas stores on your iPhone

  • Training data you enter: workouts, sets, routines, training blocks, goals, custom exercises and notes.
  • A profile photo, if you choose one in Settings with the system photo picker: Atlas keeps a small copy on your iPhone, and your profile photo and name stay on this device.
  • Workout photos you attach to a workout. You pick them with the system photo picker, so Atlas only sees the photos you choose. They are stored with the workout on your iPhone.
  • Profile details you enter: name, birth date, sex, height, body weight, max heart rate and your unit preference. Atlas uses them for heart-rate zones and to show values in your units.
  • Weigh-ins you log on the Weight screen. Atlas keeps them on your iPhone whether or not Apple Health is connected.
  • A workout in progress, so it can be resumed if the app is closed. It is deleted when the workout is saved or discarded.
  • Your settings.

This data is kept only on your iPhone.

iCloud and backups

This version of Atlas does not sync through iCloud and keeps nothing in iCloud itself. Your training does not move between iPhones on its own.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, that backup includes Atlas's data, as it does for other apps. Backups are part of iOS: you turn them on or off in iPhone Settings, and Atlas cannot see or change them.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional. Atlas works without it. When you first open Atlas you can connect Apple Health or tap "Not now", and you can connect later in Settings › Health & Devices.

Atlas asks for Health access in groups. Each group is asked only when you turn on the feature that needs it, so you never get one large request up front:

  • Core, asked when you connect Apple Health. Reads workouts, heart rate, active energy and body weight. Writes workouts, heart rate, active energy and body weight.
  • Recovery and sleep, asked when you turn on the Recovery, Sleep, Vitals or Readiness panel, or add the VO₂ max, Day strain, Sleep debt or Sleep consistency panel. Reads heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, heart-rate recovery, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, sleeping wrist temperature, sleep and VO₂ max.
  • Body, asked when you turn on Import body weight or add the Body fat panel. Reads body fat percentage, lean body mass, BMI and height.
  • Nutrition, asked when you turn on Import nutrition or add the Hydration panel. Reads dietary energy, protein, carbohydrates, fat and water.
  • Activity, asked when you turn on Import Apple Health workouts or the Readiness panel, or add the Steps panel. Reads steps, walking and running distance, and flights climbed.

Only the Core group writes to Apple Health. With your permission, Atlas saves each workout you finish to Apple Health as a strength-training workout, once. A workout you record with the Atlas Apple Watch app is saved by the Watch, with its heart rate and active energy; a workout you finish on the iPhone alone is saved by the iPhone with its start and end time. The iPhone also saves the body weight you log on the Weight screen. You can stop Atlas saving workouts in Settings › Health & Devices › Save workouts to Apple Health. When you delete a workout in Atlas, Atlas also deletes the copy it saved to Apple Health from your iPhone; a copy your Apple Watch saved stays in Apple Health, where you can delete it.

Atlas uses Health data only to show your dashboard, recovery and sleep scores, workout heart rate and calories, and your history. Some values are kept with your Atlas workouts, for example a workout's average heart rate, or a workout imported from Health. Those readings stay on your iPhone. Health data is never used for advertising and is never shared with anyone.

You can change Health access at any time in the Health app (your profile picture › Apps › Atlas) or in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Atlas. You can also disconnect Health in Atlas, in Settings › Health & Devices.

Apple Watch

The Atlas Watch app shows your routines and runs a workout on your wrist. It reads your heart rate and active energy during the workout and saves the finished workout to Apple Health, unless you turned that off or deleted the workout on your iPhone. The iPhone and the Watch send workout data directly to each other through Apple's WatchConnectivity, not through any server. The Watch keeps a copy of your routines and settings so it can start a workout on its own, and keeps a workout in progress, with any sets you log while your iPhone is out of reach, until they reach your iPhone.

Lock Screen and notifications

During a workout, Atlas can show a Live Activity on your Lock Screen with the workout name, elapsed time, rest timer and heart rate. Anyone who can see your Lock Screen can see it. You can turn it off in Settings › Training › Show on Lock Screen. Rest-timer alerts and workout reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.

AI Coach and workout summaries

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, the AI Coach and the workout summaries use Apple's on-device model. Everything is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to Atlas, Apple or anyone else.

  • The Coach builds a workout from what you type and from Atlas's built-in exercise library, and answers general training questions. It does not read your workout history, goals or Apple Health data. The conversation is not saved: it is gone when you leave the Coach screen, and the Coach keeps no notes about you. Before a message reaches the model, Atlas checks it on your iPhone for signs of an emergency, an injury or a medical question, and answers those itself with a suggestion to see a professional.
  • Workout summaries are written from your own workout numbers: the workout you just finished, compared with your earlier ones.

The Coach gives general training suggestions. It is not medical advice, and Atlas is not a medical device.

On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, the Coach screen shows sample workouts built into the app, labelled as samples, instead of using a model, and each workout summary is a short line Atlas writes from the workout's numbers, also on your iPhone.

The AI Coach and the model-written summaries are part of Atlas Pro. Without Pro, summaries are the short line Atlas writes from the workout's numbers. Either way, nothing is sent off your iPhone.

Atlas Pro purchases

Atlas Pro is an optional subscription or one-time purchase made with your Apple ID through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and Atlas never receives your payment details, your name or your Apple ID. Atlas keeps a small record on your iPhone of whether you have Pro, which plan and when it renews or ends, so Pro keeps working offline. That record is not sent anywhere, and Delete all data removes it (your purchase stays with your Apple ID and comes back on its own). If you start a free trial, Atlas can show a local notification two days before the trial ends; you can turn it off in Settings › Notifications › Trial reminder. You manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

Face ID

You can lock Atlas with Face ID in Settings › Profile & privacy. iOS handles the face scan. Atlas only learns whether you were recognized, and never sees your Face ID data.

What Atlas does not do

  • Atlas has no servers and does not upload your training, health or profile data to Atlas or anyone else.
  • Atlas has no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Atlas does not sell, rent or share your data.

Export and delete

  • Export: Settings › Data › Export workout data makes CSV files of your workouts, your sets and the weigh-ins you logged in Atlas. You choose where to save or send them with the share sheet.
  • Import: Settings › Data › Import workout data reads a Strong, Hevy or Atlas CSV file that you pick. The file is read on your iPhone and is not uploaded anywhere. Atlas keeps the imported workouts, a short list of past imports (file name and date) so you can remove one in Settings › Data › Imports, and the exercise matches you chose, so the next import doesn't ask again.
  • Delete all data: Settings › Data › Delete all data erases every workout, weigh-in, photo, routine, training block, goal, custom exercise, profile detail and setting from Atlas on this iPhone, ends any workout in progress, and returns Atlas to its welcome screen. Data Atlas already saved to Apple Health, such as workouts and weigh-ins, stays in Health, where you can delete it.
  • Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone, and deleting the Watch app removes its data from your Watch.

Children

Atlas is not directed at children under 13.
