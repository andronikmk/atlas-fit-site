Support

Something not working, or a question about Atlas? Email support@atlas-fit.fit.

It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the Atlas version (Settings › About) and what you were doing when the problem appeared. A screenshot is welcome.

Your data

Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone, and on your Apple Watch during a workout. Atlas has no accounts and no servers, and this version does not sync through iCloud. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, Atlas's data is included in that backup like any other app's.

How do I move my workouts to a new iPhone?

Restore the new iPhone from a backup of the old one and Atlas's data comes with it. You can also make a copy of your workouts yourself: Settings › Data › Export workout data saves CSV files wherever you choose.

Can I bring my history from Strong or Hevy?

Yes. Export a CSV file from that app, then in Atlas open Settings › Data › Import workout data and pick the file. It is read on your iPhone and is not uploaded anywhere. You can remove an import later in Settings › Data › Imports.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Data › Delete all data erases everything Atlas holds on that iPhone and returns it to the welcome screen. Deleting the app does the same. Workouts Atlas already saved to Apple Health stay in the Health app, where you can delete them.

Atlas Pro

I bought Pro but the app shows Free

Open Settings › Atlas Pro and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. If you subscribe and later buy Lifetime, cancel the subscription there so it does not renew.

What happens to my data if Pro ends?

Nothing is deleted or locked. You keep every workout and routine; only creating routines beyond the Free limit needs Pro.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Coach

Why does the Coach show sample workouts?

The Coach uses Apple Intelligence, which runs on your iPhone. It needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in iPhone Settings. Without it, Atlas shows built-in sample workouts, labelled as samples.

Is the Coach medical advice?

No. It gives general training suggestions, and Atlas is not a medical device. If you are injured, unwell or unsure, see a qualified professional.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

How do I connect Apple Health, or change what Atlas can read?

Connect in Settings › Health & Devices. Apple shows its permission screen only once, so to change access later open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps › Atlas.

My Today screen is empty

Recovery, sleep and the other panels are built from Apple Health data, usually recorded by an Apple Watch. If Apple Health is connected and the panels are still empty, check in the Health app that Atlas is allowed to read those types.

The Watch app is missing

Open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps and install Atlas. Keep your iPhone nearby the first time you open it so your routines can copy across.

Privacy and lock

How do I lock Atlas with Face ID?

Turn it on in Settings › Profile & privacy.

What does Atlas do with my data?

It keeps it on your iPhone. The privacy policy has the detail.
